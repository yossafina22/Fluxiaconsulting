## Services

### Intelligent websites
Professional responsive websites, chatbot, lead forms and booking integrations when configured.

### Website redesign
Modernisation of existing websites: design, mobile usability, performance, accessibility and SEO.

### AI agents
Text and voice assistants to answer questions, route enquiries and support bookings via connected systems.

### Automation
Automation of repetitive processes: email, customer enquiries, documents, orders and inventory depending on integrations.

### Instant lead response
Acknowledgement, qualification and routing of incoming leads without promising immediate human availability.

### Document processing
Extraction, classification and preparation of document data with human review for sensitive cases.

### Follow-up sequences
Structured follow-ups and lawful outreach respecting consent and opt-out rules.

### Customer reactivation
Segmentation and reactivation of existing contacts only when lawful basis and permissions are established.

### Internal reporting
Consolidation of operational data and internal dashboards/reports based on available sources.
